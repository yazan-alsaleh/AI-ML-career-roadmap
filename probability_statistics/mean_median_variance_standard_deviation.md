# Mean

There are two types of Mean:

## Population Mean μ

Its the sum of the values over the number of samples.
## Sample mean x̄

Its is when you select a small number (**only the observations in a sample** taken) from that population.


|           | Population Mean         | Sample Mean   |
| --------- | ----------------------- | ------------- |
| Data used | Entire population       | Sample        |
| Type      | **Parameter**           | **Statistic** |
| Purpose   | True population average | Estimates     |
